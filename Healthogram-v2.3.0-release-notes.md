# HEALTHOGRAM v2.3.0 RELEASE NOTES

**Release Target:** Production Version 2.3.0 (Build 23000)  
**Release Candidate Tag:** `v2.3.0-rc1`  
**Target Platform:** Android 16 (API 36) | Min SDK 24  
**Date:** September 2026  

---

### What's New in Healthogram 2.3.0

1. **Streamlined Healthcare Roles:** Clear architectural boundaries for Individuals, Doctors, Clinics, Hospitals, and Diagnostic Laboratories, with independent Marketplace Customer and Seller roles.
2. **Sovereign Health Passport:** Encrypted health records with one-time, time-limited QR code sharing and instant revocation of clinician consent.
3. **Clinical Interoperability:** Support for HL7 FHIR R4 standard clinical data exchange and Android Health Connect on-device syncing.
4. **Wellness Marketplace:** Localized health and wellness catalog with server-authoritative stock reservation and double-entry financial ledger accounting.
5. **Secure Teleconsultation:** Encrypted WebRTC audio and video calling between patients and verified clinical providers.
6. **Android 16 & Arabic Localization:** Optimized for the latest Android 16 platform with adaptive screen layouts, accessibility standards, and complete English/Arabic bilingual support.
