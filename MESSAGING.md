# HEALTHOGRAM — MESSAGING & REAL-TIME COMMUNICATIONS

## 1. Multi-Modal Communications Topology
- **Text & Media**: End-to-end encrypted messaging, media attachment, voice notes, read receipts.
- **Audio & Video Calling**: WebRTC-based calling pipeline utilizing peer-to-peer media with TURN relay fallback.
- **Privacy & Safety**: Instant user blocking, reporting, mute settings, and privacy restrictions.
- **Zero Cloud Recording Policy**: Calls are never recorded without explicit two-party consent.
