## 1. Executive Summary & Core Security Philosophy

Healthogram 2.2 enforces the strict architectural principle of **"PRIVATE BY DEFAULT"**. The Individual patient is the absolute, primary owner of their Health Passport. Healthcare organizations (Doctors, Clinics, Hospitals, Laboratories) receive zero presumptive, automatic, or ambient access. Every clinical observation, condition, allergy, medication, vital sign, and lab result is stored in an encrypted partition isolated from social feeds, advertising networks, creator commerce, and third-party tracking.

This validation confirms that version 2.2 adheres strictly to zero-trust access control, cryptographically ephemeral session tokens, immutable audit logging, and role-based data minimization.

---

## 2. Authentication & Authorization Enforcement

### 2.1 Principle of Least Privilege & Account Role Scoping
- **Strict Role Verification:** Only authenticated accounts with verified provider credentials (`DOCTOR`, `CLINIC`, `HOSPITAL`, `LABORATORY`) may initiate or receive clinical access grants.
- **Individual-to-Individual Isolation:** Patients cannot query, view, or search the Health Passports of other individual users. Any attempt yields strict denial without disclosing record existence.
- **Commercial Account Isolation:** Pharmacy (`PHARMACY`), Seller, and Creator profiles are strictly prohibited from receiving or accessing health clinical records or Health Passport timelines.

### 2.2 Identity Verification & Session Binding
- All sensitive clinical interactions require active authentication bound to verified cryptographic identities.
- Device session tokens are limited to a maximum of four concurrent active devices per identity, with automatic revocation on credential rotation or administrative invalidation.

## 3. QR-Based Access Security Architecture

### 3.1 Ephemeral Session Tokens & Anti-Replay
- **No Direct Medical Data in QR Codes:** QR payloads generated by Healthogram never contain diagnoses, lab values, prescriptions, or permanent identification numbers.
- **Cryptographic Challenge & Opaque Tokens:** The QR code encodes a single-use or short-lived opaque UUID (`qrSessionToken`) acting strictly as a handshake pointer.
- **Short Time-to-Live (TTL):** Standard clinical access QR sessions expire in **15 minutes** if unconsumed.
- **Anti-Replay Mechanism:** Once a QR token is consumed by an authorized scanner, the server atomically transitions the session state to `CONSUMED`. Re-scanning or attempting to replay the payload triggers a security alert and immediate denial (`TOKEN_ALREADY_USED`).

### 3.2 Scanning & Verification Handshake
1. **Patient Presents QR:** Generated client-side via `generateHealthQrSession` Cloud Function / local secure generator.
2. **Authorized Scanner Reads QR:** Provider submits scan request via `consumeHealthQrSession`.
3. **Identity & Scope Validation:** Gateway verifies scanner's organization registration, verified clinician license, and requested access scope (`READ_ONLY_SUMMARY`, `FULL`, `EMERGENCY`).
4. **Patient In-App Approval:** Patient receives a real-time prompt with requested categories, purpose, and duration, requiring explicit cryptographic confirmation.
5. **Session Teardown:** Once consultation concludes or TTL expires, session keys are purged.

## 4. Granular Patient Consent Engine & State Machine

### 4.1 State Machine Lifecycle
Consent grants execute through a formal, deterministic state machine:
```
[REQUESTED] ---> [APPROVED] ---> [ACTIVE] ---> [COMPLETED]
      |              |              |
      v              v              v
   [DENIED]     [CANCELLED]     [REVOKED] / [EXPIRED]
```

- **Transitions:**
  - `REQUESTED` -> `APPROVED`: Explicit patient biometric or PIN confirmation.
  - `REQUESTED` -> `DENIED`: Explicit patient rejection.
  - `APPROVED` / `ACTIVE` -> `REVOKED`: Immediate one-touch revocation by patient at any point during or after care.
  - `ACTIVE` -> `EXPIRED`: Automatic timeout when wall-clock exceeds `expiresAt`.
  - `ACTIVE` -> `COMPLETED`: Clean termination upon conclusion of clinical encounter.
- **No Implicit Grants:** All transitions are logged with actor UID, timestamp, and justification. Unrecognized or out-of-order transitions are rejected.

### 4.2 Category-Level Data Minimization
Access is strictly partitioned by category:
- `CONDITIONS`
- `ALLERGIES`
- `MEDICATIONS`
- `LAB_REPORTS`
- `VITALS`
- `PROCEDURES`

A grant specifying `LAB_REPORTS` strictly blocks access to `CONDITIONS` or `MEDICATIONS`, verified on every query at both application gateway and Firestore security rule layers.

## 5. Emergency Break-Glass & ICE Architecture

### 5.1 Lockscreen & Emergency Card Decoupling
- Healthogram provides an opt-in **Emergency Medical Card** designed for first responders and emergency personnel.
- **Decoupled Data Minimization:** The emergency payload is limited strictly to life-critical attributes:
  - Blood group
  - Severe life-threatening allergies (e.g., Anaphylaxis / Penicillin)
  - Critical life-sustaining medications (e.g., Insulin)
  - Emergency contact phone numbers
- **Zero Sensitive History:** Psychiatric notes, sexually transmitted infection screenings, reproductive health data, and granular consultation notes are **never** included in emergency payloads.

### 5.2 Break-Glass Clinical Access Protocol
- In life-threatening scenarios where the patient is incapacitated, verified hospital emergency clinicians can trigger the **Emergency Override**.
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  - Verified `HOSPITAL_EMERGENCY` or `DOCTOR` credential.
  - Formal text justification (`clinicalJustification`) logged permanently.
  - High-priority push notifications and SMS alerts dispatched to the patient and their emergency contacts immediately upon access.
  - Append-only `EmergencyAccessLog` generated and flagged for mandatory administrative review within 24 hours.

## 6. Immutable Clinical Audit Trails

### 6.1 Audit Invariants
- Every read, write, export, consent transition, and break-glass event generates an immutable audit record.
- **Append-Only Storage:** Audit collections (`health_audit_logs`, `consent_audit_logs`) prohibit updates (`allow update: if false;`) and deletions (`allow delete: if false;`).
- **Data Minimization in Audit Logs:** Audit records contain actor UID, patient UID, action, category, and timestamp, but **omit raw clinical observations or protected health information** in the audit metadata to prevent secondary leakage.
