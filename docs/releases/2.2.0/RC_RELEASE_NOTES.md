## What's New in Version 2.2.0
* **Health Passport v2 & Zero-Trust Vault:** Comprehensive end-to-end encrypted personal medical record storage (AES-GCM-256) with dynamic, single-use QR access tokens and granular, revocable clinician consents.
* **FHIR R4 Ingestion & Interoperability:** HL7 FHIR R4 9-resource mapping engine supporting Observations, Conditions, AllergyIntolerances, Immunizations, and Diagnostic Reports.
* **Health Connect Integration:** On-device biometric data syncing with local private storage.
* **Double-Entry Financial Architecture:** Immutable double-entry accounting ledger with real-time webhook deduplication, seller payouts, and sovereign platform owner revenue controls.
* **Marketplace & Courier Delivery:** Server-authoritative checkout pipeline with inventory locking, OTP-verified courier delivery handshakes, and 5-second location throttling.
* **Realtime Communications & Social:** Multi-tenant chat with ephemeral typing presence (zero permanent DB writes) and HIPAA-compliant WebRTC teleconsultation with strictly enforced no-auto-recording.
* **AI Studio Creative Suite:** Air-gapped creative media and caption generation tools with complete clinical data isolation.
* **System Stability & Android 16:** Fully optimized for Android 16 (API 36) with edge-to-edge Compose UI, 48dp touch accessibility targets, and full Arabic/RTL bidirectional rendering.
