## What's New in Healthogram v1.0.0

Healthogram makes its global debut as the premier all-in-one healthcare, personal health passport, wellness marketplace, and medical communication super-platform.

### 1. Sovereign Health Passport
- Secure personal vault for conditions, allergies, medications, and clinical documents.
- Ephemeral, single-use QR codes for instant, patient-authorized sharing with verified healthcare providers.
- Tamper-proof, immutable access logs providing complete transparency into who viewed your medical records.

### 2. Verified Healthcare Ecosystem
- Strict credential verification for Doctors, Clinics, Hospitals, and Diagnostic Laboratories.
- Granular permission boundaries ensuring laboratories cannot access non-diagnostic records and unverified accounts cannot view patient data.

### 3. Community & Social Engine
- Healthcare community feeds, short-form reels, 24-hour stories, and educational live streams.
- Advanced trust and safety moderation tools, comment filtering, and user blocking.

### 4. Wellness Marketplace & Escrow
- Curated health and wellness catalog with verified sellers.
- Protected checkout with escrow hold and 6-digit OTP delivery verification.
- Two distinct marketplace roles: Customer and Seller.

### 5. Teleconsultation & Secure Communications
- High-definition peer-to-peer WebRTC audio and video calling with zero automated recording.
- End-to-end encrypted direct messaging and real-time appointment coordination.

### 6. Privacy & Security Assurance
- Target SDK 36 compliant.
- Strict 4-device simultaneous session limit per account.
- Zero-trust Firebase security architecture with Google Play Integrity attestation.
