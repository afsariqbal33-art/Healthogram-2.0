## 3. Full Description
```text
Healthogram is a comprehensive digital health and wellness platform designed to empower individuals with sovereign control over their health data while providing seamless connectivity to healthcare providers and wellness essentials.

KEY FEATURES:

• Health Passport: Maintain your personal health record with encrypted storage of conditions, medications, allergies, vitals, and immunization histories. Your medical data remains private by default under client-side encryption.

• Sovereign Provider Sharing: Share selected health records with doctors, clinics, hospitals, and diagnostic laboratories using single-use, time-limited QR codes. You maintain full visibility of access logs and can revoke consent at any moment.

• Verified Healthcare Directory: Discover licensed doctors, outpatient clinics, general hospitals, and diagnostic laboratories. View provider credentials, book appointments, and attend scheduled teleconsultation calls.

• Wellness Marketplace: Browse approved health, fitness, and wellness products from verified sellers. Track orders from checkout to delivery with transparent order management.

• Health Community: Engage in meaningful health discussions, share wellness journeys, and connect with health communities through secure social feeds and educational media.

• Multilingual Support: Full support for English and Arabic with native Right-to-Left (RTL) formatting.

DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY:
Healthogram is built on a privacy-first, zero-trust architecture. We believe your health information belongs exclusively to you. Your Health Passport data is protected by client-side envelope encryption (AES-GCM-256) and is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for automated AI model training.

IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:
Healthogram is not a medical device (Software as a Medical Device - SaMD) and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or emergency services. The content and features provided in Healthogram are for informational and organizational purposes only. Always consult a licensed physician or qualified healthcare provider with any questions regarding a medical condition. In case of a medical emergency, call your local emergency medical services immediately.
```
