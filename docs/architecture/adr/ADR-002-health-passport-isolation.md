# ADR-002: ZERO-TRUST HEALTH PASSPORT AIR-GAP & ISOLATION

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Deciders:** Healthcare Data Security Architect, Privacy Engineer, Principal Architect  

---

## 1. Context
The Health Passport stores highly confidential, protected health information (PHI) including medical diagnoses, laboratory reports, allergy registers, and prescription records. Commercial platform components (marketplace recommendations, social ads, generative AI models) operate concurrently within Healthogram.

## 2. Problem
How do we mathematically guarantee that sensitive clinical records in the Health Passport are never exposed to unauthorized providers, commercial advertising systems, AI training pipelines, or social recommendation engines?

## 3. Options Considered
- **Option A: Unified Document Store with Row-Level Access:** Store health records in the general user document and rely on standard app security rules.
- **Option B: Total Physical Air-Gap in Separate VPC:** Deploy an entirely separate healthcare infrastructure in an isolated cloud provider.
- **Option C: Sovereign Cryptographic Vault with Ephemeral Token Handshake (Selected):** Isolate health collections within dedicated Firestore security boundaries, encrypt sensitive fields with AES-GCM-256 keys managed in Cloud KMS, enforce patient-controlled single-use QR grants (15-minute TTL), and maintain an immutable access log.

## 4. Decision
Adopt **Option C: Sovereign Cryptographic Vault with Ephemeral Token Handshake**. Health Passport collections are sealed with default-deny rules. Access requires an ephemeral cryptographic token generated by the patient and validated against strict healthcare provider verification records.

## 5. Reason
This provides zero-trust security without the immense operational fragmentation of running two completely separate cloud backends. Even if an attacker gains read access to general user documents, Health Passport clinical records remain fully encrypted and inaccessible.

## 6. Tradeoffs
- Healthcare providers cannot search or access patient records without an explicit, active patient handshake.
- Ephemeral tokens require real-time clock synchronization (NTP) between devices and backend.

## 7. Consequences
- Generic analytics, social feeds, and AI models have ZERO read or query permissions on `health_*` collections.
- All access requests, approvals, scans, and revocations are permanently logged to `health_access_logs`.
- Patient sovereignty is preserved at all times with one-tap revocation capability.
