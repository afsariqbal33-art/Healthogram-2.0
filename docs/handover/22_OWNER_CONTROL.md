# 22 — OWNER CONTROL PANEL & PLATFORM GOVERNANCE

## 1. Executive Operations & Authority
The Owner Control Panel provides root platform governance accessible only to authorized platform owners with mandatory hardware key or multi-factor biometric step-up authentication.

## 2. Governed Operational Domains
* **Fee Structure Management:** Setting platform commission percentage (e.g. 10%), seller transaction fee caps, and escrow hold durations.
* **Country Activation Controls:** Enabling or disabling specific country jurisdictions for marketplace commerce or clinical services.
* **Global Maintenance Modes:** Activating scheduled maintenance overlays across web and mobile clients.
* **Emergency Subsystem Isolation:** Triggering real-time circuit breakers for payments, AI, calling, or delivery without rebuilding mobile APKs.

## 3. Dual-Control Requirement for High-Risk Actions
Any change to financial fee schedules, payout parameters, or global kill switches requires explicit dual-operator authorization (four-eyes principle) before taking effect in production.
