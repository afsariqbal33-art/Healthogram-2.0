# 09 — HEALTHCARE ORGANIZATIONS & CLINICAL WORKFLOWS

## 1. Role-Specific Clinical Dashboards
Healthogram provides specialized clinical interfaces for each of the four accredited healthcare organization categories:

### A. Doctor Workflow
* **My Appointments:** Daily and weekly consultation schedule with status toggles (In-Person / Video Call).
* **Patient Consultation Mode:** Consent-gated record viewer (vitals, medications, previous notes).
* **Clinical Prescriptions:** Issue standardized digital prescriptions directly to the patient's Health Passport vault.

### B. Clinic Workflow
* **Facility Management:** Multi-doctor department scheduling and specialty management (e.g. Pediatrics, Cardiology).
* **Slot Allocation:** Dynamic schedule templates and holiday exclusion calendars.
* **Receptionist Check-In:** Validates patient appointment confirmations and consent tokens.

### C. Hospital Workflow
* **Inpatient & Emergency Roster:** Centralized roster covering multiple buildings, wards, and clinical staff.
* **Referral Management:** In-network referrals between hospital specialists.

### D. Laboratory Workflow
* **Test Catalog Management:** Definition of blood panels, imaging packages, and turnaround times.
* **Specimen Intake & Status:** Barcode sample tracking (Received, Processing, Completed).
* **Direct Encrypted Result Reporting:** Uploads signed diagnostic PDF reports directly into the patient's encrypted vault.

## 2. Inviolable Governance Rule: No Direct Laboratory Health Passport
* Laboratories perform specimen diagnostic analysis only. A laboratory account **never** possesses an independent personal Health Passport.
* Laboratory access to patient history is strictly restricted to tests relevant to the active requisition.
