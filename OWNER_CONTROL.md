# HEALTHOGRAM — OWNER CONTROL & EARNINGS

## 1. Owner Control System
- **Emergency Kill Switch**: Immediate platform-wide disablement in case of security or regulatory incident.
- **Maintenance Mode**: Restricts platform access while keeping critical core Health Passport emergency lookup online.
- **Multi-Level Feature Flags**: Enable/disable features globally, per country, per account type, or per user.

## 2. Owner Earnings & Financial Ledger
- **Gross Platform Volume**: Aggregation of marketplace transactions, consultations, and subscriptions.
- **Commission Ledger**: Real-time commission ledger entries.
- **Audit-Protected Balances**: Net platform revenue and pending settlement balances derived from immutable ledger transactions.
