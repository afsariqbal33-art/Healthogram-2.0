# HEALTHOGRAM — UNIVERSAL LANGUAGE TRANSLATION

## 1. Scope & Availability
- Available to all users across text messaging, voice messages, audio calls, and video calls.
- Supports English, Arabic (with full RTL support), Spanish, French, German, Hindi, and Chinese.

## 2. Mandatory Clinical Disclaimer
*NOTICE*: Universal translation is an automated accessibility aid and must not be represented as certified clinical interpretation. Critical medical consultations must utilize certified medical interpreters.
