# HEALTHOGRAM — AI STUDIO SPECIFICATION

## 1. Operating Modes
1. **Creator Mode**: Image retouching, auto-caption generation, smart hashtag recommendation, video clipping.
2. **Seller Mode**: Product photo enhancement, background removal, title and search tag generation, marketing banners.
3. **Healthcare Organization Mode**: Public health notice formatting, health wellness campaigns, service announcements.

## 2. Ethical Invariant
- Generative AI is strictly forbidden from fabricating medical diagnoses, synthesizing treatment prescriptions, or receiving patient Health Passport data.
